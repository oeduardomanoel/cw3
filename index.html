What we design

Six disciplines.
One team that answers the phone.

Plenty of projects stall in the handoff between the roadway firm, the drainage firm and the utility firm. We do all of it in house, so the plans line up before they ever reach the reviewer.

  1. Neighborhood streets, major arterials and interstate systems, including utility work on the Interstate 75 and Colonial Boulevard improvements for FDOT District One. We also lay out the sidewalks and shared use paths people actually want to walk on.

    • Minor roadway
    • Major roadway
    • Interstate systems
    • Sidewalks & shared use paths

  2. Water mains, force mains, reuse and gravity sewer. We handle FDOT UWHCA agreements too, which is how we ended up designing relocations for Lee County Utilities and the City of Fort Myers on one of the busiest interchanges in the region.

    • Water main
    • Force main
    • Reuse
    • Gravity sewer
    • FDOT UWHCA

  3. Water is the whole story in this part of Florida. We model open and closed systems, size the stormwater management, run nutrient loading analysis and carry the Environmental Resource Permit through review. When a project sits in a floodplain, we prepare the FEMA No Rise certification.

    • Hydraulic analysis & design
    • Stormwater management
    • Nutrient loading
    • ERP permitting
    • FEMA No Rise

  4. Residential, commercial and industrial sites, from a bank branch in Marathon to algae farm sites covering hundreds of acres. Dewatering and water use permits with SFWMD are part of the package, so your contractor is not waiting on paperwork.

    • Residential
    • Commercial
    • Industrial
    • Dewatering & water use permits

  5. Everything on the ground side of the fence: access roads, utilities and site work for airport facilities. Lee County Port Authority is on our client list, and we know how much coordination an active airport demands.

    • Transportation
    • Utilities
    • Site design

  6. Plenty of firms hand over signed plans and disappear. We stay through construction with full time engineering and inspection, like the year we spent on the Linhart neighborhood utility upgrades for the City of Fort Myers.

    • Transportation
    • Utilities
    • Site design

How a project moves

Four stages. The same people from start to finish.

01

Scope it honestly

We walk the site, pull the records and tell you what the project really needs. If something will cost you time with the county, you hear it from us in week one.

02

Design it to pass review

Roadway, drainage and utility plans come off the same desks, so the profiles match and the conflicts get solved on paper instead of in the trench.

03

Carry the permits

ERP, water use, dewatering, FEMA No Rise, FDOT agreements. We know what reviewers in these counties tend to flag, and we fix it before the first submittal.

04

Stay through construction

Our CEI team answers RFIs, inspects the work and signs the certifications. The engineer who drew the plan is a phone call away when the field finds a surprise.
